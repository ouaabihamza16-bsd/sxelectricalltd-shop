About us

An independent electrical company in Wickford

SX ELECTRICAL LTD is an independent, owner-run electrical installation company based in Wickford, Essex. The company was incorporated in July 2015 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 09676835, with electrical installation as its registered business activity.

Our work is electrical installation and the services that go with it: putting in new wiring, circuits, sockets and lighting, altering or updating existing installations, and tracing and repairing electrical faults.

As a small company, we keep things plain and practical: a clear conversation about what is needed, then the work itself.

Services

Electrical installation and related work

A short overview of the kind of electrical work we carry out.

Electrical installation

New wiring and circuits, installed to suit the property and how it will be used.

Lighting and sockets

Adding or moving lighting points, switches and socket outlets.

Alterations and upgrades

Extending, altering or updating existing electrical installations, including older wiring.

Fault finding and repairs

Tracing trips, faults and circuits that have stopped working, then making safe and repairing.
